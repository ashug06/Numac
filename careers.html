Build your career with Numac

We are building a growing healthcare organisation and welcome people who value responsibility, learning, collaboration and meaningful work.

What we look for

Scientific honesty

Detailing that matches the label. We would rather lose a call than invent a claim.

Respect for the trade

Pharmacists, stockists and doctors keep Indian healthcare moving. We treat them as partners.

Sustainable effort

Field work is demanding. We still expect people to take leave, recover and return clear-headed.
