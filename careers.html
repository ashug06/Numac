People

Work that leaves room for a life

Our vision includes a family atmosphere for employees so they can enjoy their work and spend quality time at home. We are a growing Bengaluru company, not a faceless giant — which means your contribution is visible, and so is your evening.

What we look for

Scientific honesty

Detailing that matches the label. We would rather lose a call than invent a claim.

Respect for the trade

Pharmacists, stockists, and doctors keep Indian healthcare moving. We treat them as partners, not targets.

Stamina without burnout theatre

Field work is demanding. We still expect people to take leave, recover, and come back clear-headed.
