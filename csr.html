CSR

Healthier families, starting with our own

CSR at Numac is a continuing commitment to behave ethically and contribute to economic and social development while improving the quality of life of the workforce and their families, as well as the local community and society at large.

Where we put the effort

Access to therapy

Affordable pricing is our first social act. A well-made generic that a family can complete the course on is worth more than a donated camp that runs out of stock.

Workforce and families

We design roles so employees can spend time at home. Health cover conversations, leave, and dignity on the field are part of CSR, not extras.

Local community

As a Malleshwaram-based company we prefer practical local support — health awareness with clinicians, and partnerships that do not exploit patient data for publicity.

We do not publish staged photographs of patients. If you represent a hospital, NGO, or panchayat and want a medicines or awareness partnership, use the contact form.
