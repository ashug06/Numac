About Numac

An established healthcare company with modern standards

Joining Hands for Excellent Health. Numac Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. is a Bengaluru pharmaceutical company established in 2012, focused on quality products and trusted healthcare partnerships.

Who we are

Numac markets a selected pharmaceutical and nutraceutical portfolio for use as directed by registered medical practitioners. Our work sits with doctors, pharmacies, distributors and manufacturing partners rather than as an online pharmacy.

The current range spans infection, gastroenterology, dermatology, nutrition, neuro and related areas. Pack inserts remain the authority for composition, indication and use.
