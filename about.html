Who we are

Numac Healthcare is a company with a clear therapeutic focus on practical care solutions. The approved product range in the company brief is built around hair care, gastroenterology, neuro support, and dermatology.

Our intention is simple: support physicians with quality formulations and help patients access products that are easy to understand, easy to trust, and aligned with the care journey.
