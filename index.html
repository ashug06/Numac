Why Numac

A professional approach to healthcare brands

We market a focused range with an emphasis on quality, responsibility and long-term partnerships — not volume claims or promotional language.

Quality

A consistent focus on dependable pharmaceutical products and pack information that matches the labelled specification.

Partnerships

Long-term relationships across the healthcare ecosystem — clinicians, trade partners and manufacturing sites.

Responsibility

A professional approach to claims, safety reporting and the use of medicines only as directed by a registered practitioner.

Growth

Building the organisation through sustainable partnerships rather than short-term shortcuts.

Manufacturing

Manufacturing through trusted partners

Numac works with selected manufacturing partners to support quality, consistency and reliable supply across its portfolio.

Like many focused Indian marketing houses, we do not claim to own every plant. Partner facilities are selected based on applicable regulatory, quality and manufacturing requirements. Numac remains responsible for the brands that carry our name.

Manufacturing partners

How we work with plants

Tablets, capsules, syrups, injectables and dermatologicals are sourced through contract-manufacturing and related arrangements, with documentation and complaint handling retained by Numac.

Certification and licence status belong to the manufacturing partner for each product. We do not display generic quality badges on this website in place of plant-specific documentation.
