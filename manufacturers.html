Contract manufacturing

Third-party manufacturers we are proud to stand beside

Numac is a marketing-led company. Every pack that carries our name is produced in qualified plants — WHO-GMP, Schedule M, and ISO-oriented partners we audit, document, and hold to the same patient promise we make in the clinic.

How the model works

Indian clinicians already know this structure: a focused brand house designs the portfolio, a licensed manufacturer produces it, and a field force takes it to doctors. We are explicit about that, because opacity helps no one — not regulators, not partners, not patients.

We engage plants on loan-licence, contract-manufacturing, and principal-to-principal arrangements depending on the molecule and the market. Numac retains brand, artwork, pharmacovigilance, and commercial responsibility.

  1. Need and dossier. Therapeutic gap, composition, and target presentation are defined with medical and regulatory input.
  2. Plant qualification. Licence, GMP certificate, capacity, and quality-system review before any purchase order.
  3. Tech transfer. Specifications, in-process controls, and stability expectations are locked with the quality unit.
  4. Commercial supply. Batch release, artwork control, and complaint handling run for the life of the brand.

Non-negotiable standards

Licences in force

Manufacturing licence, GMP certificate, and product permission for the exact composition and pack. No “nearby equivalent” substitutions without a variation.

Quality system

Deviation, OOS, change control, and recall procedures that we can inspect. A plant that cannot show a recall drill is not a partner.

People and environment

We expect ethical labour practice and environmental permits. CSR is not a brochure line if the shop floor contradicts it.
