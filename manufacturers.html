Manufacturing

Manufacturing through trusted partners

Numac works with selected manufacturing partners to support quality, consistency and reliable supply across its portfolio. Partner facilities are selected based on applicable regulatory, quality and manufacturing requirements.

How the model works

Numac is a marketing-led company. A focused brand house defines the portfolio; a licensed manufacturer produces it; a field and trade network takes it to healthcare professionals. We are explicit about that structure.

Arrangements may include contract manufacturing and related commercial models depending on the product. Numac retains brand, artwork, pharmacovigilance and commercial responsibility for products marketed in our name.

  1. Need and dossier. Therapeutic gap, composition and presentation are defined with medical and regulatory input.
  2. Plant qualification. Licence, capacity and quality-system review before commercial supply.
  3. Tech transfer. Specifications and in-process controls are agreed with the quality unit.
  4. Commercial supply. Batch release, artwork control and complaint handling continue for the life of the brand.

What we expect of partners

Licences in force

Manufacturing licence and product permission for the exact composition and pack. Substitutions require a documented variation.

Quality system

Deviation, change control and recall procedures that can be inspected. Certification belongs to the plant, not to a generic website badge.

People and environment

Ethical labour practice and environmental permits expected as part of ordinary due diligence.
